QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked.

Answers to common questions about the service and workflow.

Who can use MigrationAdmin services?+

MigrationAdmin is designed as B2B support for migration professionals and related practitioners.

Do you provide immigration advice?+

No. The service is administrative, drafting and research support for professional review.

What is the typical turnaround?+

Most listed standard services are around 5–7 business days, with some specialist work taking longer.

How do I submit a case?+

Start with the enquiry form or a discovery call and the team will guide you through the case details.

Can I request revisions?+

Yes. The RMA review stage is where comments and revisions can be addressed before final delivery.

Is information kept confidential?+

Confidential handling is part of the service and client-contact protections.
