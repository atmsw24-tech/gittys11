GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently
Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about our services.

Who can use MigrationAdmin services? +

MigrationAdmin provides B2B administrative and drafting support primarily for migration professionals, registered migration agents and legal practitioners.

Do you provide immigration advice? +

No. MigrationAdmin provides administrative, copywriting and research support only. Professional responsibility remains with the relevant RMA or legal practitioner.

What is your typical turnaround time? +

Most services are completed within 5–7 business days. Some services, such as National Innovation Visa portfolios, generally require approximately two weeks.

How do I submit documents for my case? +

After your enquiry and engagement are confirmed, we'll provide instructions for supplying the required case information and documents.

What if I need revisions to the draft? +

Your matter goes through an internal review process before final delivery. Revision requirements can be discussed as part of the drafting process.

Is my client's information secure? +

Confidentiality is a core part of our service. Information supplied for drafting purposes is treated as confidential.
